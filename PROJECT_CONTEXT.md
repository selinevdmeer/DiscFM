# DiscFM

DiscFM is an application for music lovers and record collectors. The app helps users compare their most-listened-to albums with the albums in their Discogs library.

## Core Features

- Compare albums the user has listened to with albums in their Discogs library.
- Allow users to add albums they have listened to directly to their Discogs wantlist.

## Product Context

The comparison brings together the user's listening habits and record collection. This makes it easy to see which of their favorite albums are already in their Discogs library and which ones might still be missing. The wantlist feature allows users to save discovered or previously listened-to albums directly to their wantlist, making it easier to find and collect them later.
